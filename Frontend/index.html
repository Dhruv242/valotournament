How It Works

From sign-up to lobby code, in 8 quick steps.

  • 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with a unique username, your Indian mobile number, and a password.

  • 2

    Build your squad

    Name your team and pick the Valorant agent that represents it.

  • 3

    Add players

    Add up to 6 players with their in-game Valorant name and tag. The 6th player is your standby — if you don't have one yet, fill in any placeholder (e.g., jvbwjg#fhe3oifh).

  • 4

    Pick a tier

    Amateur (₹250 entry, ₹1,000 prize) or Pro (₹500 entry, ₹2,000 prize).

  • 5

    Pay via UPI

    Scan our QR with any UPI app — GPay, PhonePe, Paytm — and pay your entry fee in seconds.

  • 6

    Submit your UTR

    Paste the 12-digit reference from your UPI app. We cross-check it against our bank and confirm your slot.

  • 7

    Wait for the bracket to fill

    Tournaments lock once 8 teams have paid in. Your dashboard shows your slot in real time.

  • 8

    Get your party ID

    Your Valorant custom-lobby code appears on your dashboard 30 minutes before your first match. Join the lobby, play, win.

Why You Can Trust Us

Built for the community, transparent end-to-end.

  • Verified UPI payments

    Every payment is cross-checked against our bank statement before your tournament slot is confirmed.

  • Refund if the bracket doesn't fill

    If your tournament doesn't fill 8 teams within 7 days of your payment, you get a full refund — no questions.

  • No DMs asking for money

    We will never message you outside this site asking for payment. Pay only via the QR code shown on the registration page.

  • Skill-based, no luck involved

    Valorant is a skill game. Prize pools come from team entries plus our own contribution — no chance, no dice.

  • Age and state eligibility

    18+ only. Participation is restricted in Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Assam, Odisha, Nagaland, and Sikkim per state law.

  • Direct support

    Something off? Reach us at support@valotournament.in — we reply within a day.
